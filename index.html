📖 How to Use TradeSim Pro

1. Explore the Market

Go to the Market tab to view live simulated prices. Click on any stock name to view its detailed Growth Chart and Company Fundamentals.

2. How to Buy Stocks

Click the BUY button on any stock card. Enter the quantity and confirm.

3. Track Your Portfolio

Navigate to the Portfolio tab. Monitor the performance of your investments in real-time.

4. How to Sell Stocks

Click the SELL button from your Portfolio to book profit.
