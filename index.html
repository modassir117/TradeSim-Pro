📖 How to Use TradeSim Pro

1. Explore the Market

Go to the Market tab to view simulated stock prices. Click a stock name to see its chart and fundamentals.

2. Buy Stocks

Click BUY on any stock card, enter quantity and confirm the order.

3. Track Portfolio

Open Portfolio to see invested amount, current value and profit/loss.

4. Sell Stocks

Go to Portfolio and click SELL to sell your holdings.
